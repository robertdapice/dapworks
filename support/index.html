Sage

Getting started

  • Install Sage and set up your profile so targets are personalised.
  • Log a meal with a photo or by voice. Review the estimate and adjust anything that looks off.
  • Check the dashboard for today's projection and your protein target.

Troubleshooting

  • Photo logging isn't recognising food. Use a clear, well-lit photo taken from above. Retake from a different angle if needed, then edit the entry if the estimate is still wrong.
  • Voice logging isn't working. Check Sage has microphone permission in Settings, speak clearly, and make sure you have an internet connection.
  • The app crashes or won't open. Restart your iPhone and update to the latest version of Sage.
  • Delete my data. Email us from the address linked to your account and we'll delete it.

Rep

Getting started

  • Rep needs full photo library access to find your handstand history. Limited access won't find older clips.
  • The first scan runs in the foreground and can take several minutes on large libraries. Keep the app open.

Troubleshooting

  • A rep was missed or mislabelled. Swipe on the clip to correct the movement or crop. Corrections stay on your device.
  • Someone I don't want in the app appeared. Tap the person card and choose Hide.
  • Remove everything. Deleting Rep removes all its data. Your original videos are never modified.
