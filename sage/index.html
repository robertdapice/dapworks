Sage · iOS

Track macros in seconds.

Sage is a macro and calorie tracker built for people who are tired of spending too long logging food. Take a photo, or say what you ate, and Sage does the rest.

What Sage does

  • Photo-based food loggingCapture meals quickly without searching long food lists.
  • Voice-based food loggingLog naturally while you're on the go.
  • Fast edit and correctionFix an entry in seconds when the estimate needs adjusting.
  • Daily macro and calorie projectionSee where your day is trending before it ends.
  • Protein-first targetsKeep attention on the nutrition target that matters most.
  • Clean daily dashboardUnderstand progress at a glance.
  • Designed for minimal tapsLess tracking fatigue, more consistency.
  • Recent historyReview patterns and adjust habits with less guesswork.

Who it's for

People who already know macro tracking from apps like MyFitnessPal or Cal AI, care about protein and calorie awareness, and dislike tedious data entry. Sage supports nutrition awareness without the clutter. It is not a medical product and doesn't make weight-loss guarantees.

Privacy

Photos and voice clips you log are used to estimate nutrition and are stored with your account so you can see your history. We don't sell your data or share it without your permission. Details are in the Dapworks privacy policy.
