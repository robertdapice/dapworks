Sage

Smart Macro Tracker · Track macros in seconds

  • iOS 17+
  • Launching soon on the App Store
  • Early access: hello@dapworks.co

Take a photo of your meal, or say what you ate. Sage estimates the calories and macros, keeps a running total for the day, and puts protein first.

Features

  • Photo loggingSnap a meal. Sage identifies the food and estimates macros.
  • Voice loggingSay "chicken wrap and a flat white". Done.
  • Quick editsAdjust any estimate in a couple of taps.
  • Daily projectionSee where calories and protein will land before the day ends.
  • Protein-first targetsProtein sits next to calories on every screen.
  • Weigh-ins and weekly reviewLog weight, get a weekly summary of intake against trend.

Privacy

Photos and voice clips you log are used to estimate nutrition and stored with your account. Not sold, not shared without permission. Full detail in the privacy policy. Support is at /support.
