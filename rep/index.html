Rep

The handstand app.

  • In development
  • Early access: hello@dapworks.co

Rep scans the videos already on your phone, finds the handstands, crops each one to the rep, and charts hold time by session.

  • Finds the repsPose detection picks handstand holds, presses and push-ups out of your library.
  • Crops to the repEach attempt becomes its own clip. Originals are untouched.
  • Hold timeSeconds on hands per rep, per session, and over time.
  • Tags and filtersStraight, tuck, press, wall, spotter.
  • Compare and shareTwo reps side by side, or a date-stamped montage.
  • On deviceNo video, pose or face data leaves the phone.

Data

Rep reads your photo library on the device. Nothing is uploaded. Deleting the app removes everything it stored. Details in the privacy policy.
