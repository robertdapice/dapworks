Every handstand you've ever filmed, found and organised automatically.

Rep scans the videos already on your iPhone, detects handstand holds, presses and push-ups, crops each clip to the single rep, and lays them out in a timeline with hold duration charted over time. Nothing to log. Nothing uploaded.

Rep

What it does

  • Finds the repsOn-device pose detection picks handstand work out of thousands of clips.
  • Crops to the repEach attempt becomes its own clip. Originals are never modified.
  • Charts hold timeDuration per rep, plotted across months, so progress is visible at a glance.
  • Timelines per personYou, training partners, or clients if you coach.
  • Compare and exportSide-by-side playback of any two reps, and date-stamped journey montages.
  • Private by designVideo, poses and people data stay on the phone.

Detects

  • Handstand hold, wall and freestanding, with hold duration
  • Handstand press, straddle and pike
  • Handstand push-up

Privacy

Rep asks for full photo library access so it can find older clips. All analysis runs on your device. See the privacy policy and support.
