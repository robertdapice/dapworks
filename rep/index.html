The handstand app.

Every handstand you've ever filmed, found and organised automatically. Rep scans your photo library, finds the handstand work, crops each video to the individual rep, and builds a timeline of your progress.

Why Rep exists

Handstand practitioners film constantly. The footage exists, buried in a camera roll among thousands of other clips. The job isn't capture, it's organisation. Rep's rule is that anything requiring work at filming time or logging time is out of scope. Your history should assemble itself.

What Rep does

  • Finds the reps for youRep scans videos already on your phone and detects handstand holds, presses, and handstand push-ups.
  • Crops to the repEach clip is trimmed to the individual attempt. Your originals are never modified.
  • Charts hold durationHold time is a first-class metric, plotted over time so you can see months of progress at once.
  • Timelines per personYou, training partners, or clients if you coach. Everyone gets their own chronological timeline.
  • Compare and sharePlay any two reps side by side, or export a date-stamped journey montage.
  • Everything on-deviceDetection runs on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded.

Privacy

Rep needs access to your photo library to find handstand videos. All analysis happens on your device. Videos, poses, and people data never leave your phone, and you can delete Rep's data at any time. See the privacy policy.
