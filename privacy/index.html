Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated 18 September 2026

Dapworks ("we", "us") builds iOS apps and runs this website. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and what control you have. We collect the minimum we need, and we don't sell personal information.

This website

dapworks.co and dapworks.com.au are static sites. We don't use cookies, analytics, or tracking. The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, whose servers may log standard request data such as IP address for security and operational purposes under GitHub's privacy statement.

Email

If you email hello@dapworks.co, we keep your message and address so we can reply and follow up. We don't add you to any mailing list.

Sage

Sage estimates the nutrition of food you log.

  • What we collect. Meal photos, voice recordings or transcripts, the food entries and edits you make, your profile details such as targets, and an account identifier so your history syncs to your device.
  • How we use it. Photos and voice inputs are sent to our servers and to third-party AI providers to estimate the foods and macros in a meal. Entries and history are stored with your account so you can see trends. We use this data only to run and improve Sage.
  • Sharing. We share data with the service providers that host Sage and perform the nutrition estimate, under contracts that limit use to providing that service. We don't share it with anyone else without your permission.
  • Retention and deletion. Data is kept while you have an account. You can delete entries in the app, and you can ask us to delete your account and all associated data by emailing hello@dapworks.co.

Rep

Rep finds handstand videos in your photo library and organises them.

  • What we collect. Nothing leaves your device. Rep reads your photo library locally to detect movement and, optionally, group clips by person.
  • How it's processed. All video analysis, pose detection, and face grouping run on your iPhone. Rep never modifies your original videos and doesn't upload video, images, poses, or people data.
  • Retention and deletion. Rep's index, corrections, and person groups are stored on your device only and are removed when you delete the app. Hiding a person removes their group from Rep.

Children

Our apps aren't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Security

Data in transit is encrypted. Access to production systems is limited to the people who need it to run the service.

Your rights

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, or complain about how we've handled it. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles. Contact us at hello@dapworks.co.

Changes

We'll update this page if our practices change and note the date at the top.
