Privacy

Updated 1 October 2026

Dapworks builds apps and runs this website. This page says what we collect and why. We collect the minimum and sell nothing.

Website

dapworks.co is a static site with no cookies, analytics or tracking. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log request data such as IP addresses under GitHub's privacy statement.

Email

If you email us we keep the message so we can reply. There is no mailing list.

Sage

  • Collected: meal photos, voice recordings or transcripts, food entries and edits, profile details such as targets, and an account identifier.
  • Used for: estimating the food and macros in a meal, and keeping your history. Photos and voice inputs are sent to our servers and to third-party AI providers for the estimate.
  • Shared with: the providers that host Sage and produce the estimate, under contracts limited to that purpose. Nobody else without your permission.
  • Kept: while you have an account. Delete entries in the app, or email hello@dapworks.co to delete the account and all data.

Rep

  • Collected: nothing leaves the device. Rep reads your photo library locally to find handstands.
  • Processed: video analysis and pose detection run on the phone. Originals are never modified.
  • Kept: on the device only. Deleting the app removes it.

Children

Our apps are not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Your rights

Ask what we hold about you, or ask for it to be corrected or deleted, at hello@dapworks.co. We handle personal information under the Australian Privacy Principles.
