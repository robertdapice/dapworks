About

Dapworks

Dapworks is an independent iOS studio in Sydney, Australia, founded by Rob Dapice. It builds Sage and Rep. Both apps started as tools for the founder's own training and nutrition.

The approach is the same in each: one job per app, automation instead of data entry, and on-device processing wherever possible.
