About Dapworks

Dapworks is an independent software studio based in Sydney, Australia. It was founded by Rob Dapice to build small, focused apps that solve one problem properly.

The studio's first products are Sage, a macro tracker that logs meals from a photo or a sentence, and Rep, an app that finds and organises every handstand video you've ever filmed. Both started as tools built for the founder's own use and grew from there.

What we believe

  • Zero effort is the product. Automation beats data entry. If the user has to work to feed the app, the app has failed.
  • One clear magic moment. Every product is designed around a single moment that should land within minutes of install.
  • Private by default. Process on-device where possible, collect the minimum otherwise, and say plainly what leaves your phone.
  • Plain language. No hype, no overpromising. We describe what the software does.
